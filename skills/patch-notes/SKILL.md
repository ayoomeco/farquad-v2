# Patch notes

Use when the owner asks what changed, or after a real revision of the recipe, a skill, the baseline or the code.

Write human patch notes. Three buckets only:

- **Changed**: what got rewritten and why, in a sentence
- **Removed**: what got cut
- **Added**: what showed up

No process diary. Keep it short enough to read in chat, and also drop a dated file next to the work (for Bill: `$FMB_HOME/docs/PATCH_NOTES.md`, newest entry on top, plus a copy in the job's Dropbox folder).

Check the patch notes, knowledge base and job index BEFORE re-asking the owner anything already decided. They exist so nothing gets forgotten.
